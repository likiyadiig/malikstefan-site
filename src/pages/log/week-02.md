turned it into a product.

## What broke

I thought the problem was formatting and house style. It isn't.

different way: the AI doesn't follow what came before. One
person said she has to keep retraining it, another said it
doesn't keep up with changes they'd already worked on
together, another said he has to repeat instructions when the
background should already be there.
