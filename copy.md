## 5. Questions

Heading: Questions

Q: Will it change what my report says?
A: It tries not to. Every edit is a tracked change you can accept or reject, and edits that might change the meaning, like a changed number or a removed "may", get a comment so you check them first.

Q: Which files can I upload?
A: Word files (.docx), up to 25 MB.

Q: Can I use my own style rules?
A: Yes. Write them in your own words on the Style rules page, or start from the default rules.

Q: Where is my report stored, and who sees it?
A: On my server in New York, USA. The text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to make the edits. Files are kept until you ask me to delete them. Details are on the privacy page (link to /privacy).

Q: How do I cancel?
A: Click Manage subscription on the billing page. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
